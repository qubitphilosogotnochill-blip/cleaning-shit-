Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before booking? Here is everything you need to know about our service and policies.

Do you provide your own cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes, entirely! Our cleaning professionals bring HEPA-filtered vacuums, clean microfiber mop systems, and eco-friendly, pet-safe disinfectants. If you have specialty surfaces requiring dedicated products (such as unsealed natural stone), simply leave them on the counter with instructions.
Do I need to be at home while the cleaners work?
It is completely up to your preference. Many clients provide digital door codes, key lockbox combinations, or buzz us in through front desk concierge. All cleaners are background-checked, insured, and bonded so you can go about your day with total peace of mind.
What is your 24-hour satisfaction guarantee?
Your happiness is our priority. If any corner, fixture, or room doesn't meet your expectations, let us know within 24 hours of completion and we will dispatch a team member to re-clean the area free of charge.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
You can reschedule or cancel any appointment free of charge up to 24 hours prior to the arrival window. Late cancellations within 24 hours may incur a modest fee to compensate our cleaners for their reserved schedule.
Are your cleaning detergents pet safe?
Yes, our standard cleaning formulas are non-toxic, cruelty-free, and plant-derived. We simply request that active pets are placed in a secure room while vacuuming for their comfort and safety.
